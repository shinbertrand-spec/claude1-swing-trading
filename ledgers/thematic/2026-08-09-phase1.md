## 1. The binding-constraint call

**Current (evidence dated 2026-07-16, TD Economics, read in full): the
electric-power delivery chain** — four sub-constraints gating each other:
gas-turbine slots (orders ~110 GW/yr vs 60–70 GW/yr global capacity; GEV
sold out through 2028), grid interconnection (3–4 yr queues; 7 yr NoVA),
transformers/switchgear (2–5 yr leads, +77% price vs pre-pandemic, leads
still LENGTHENING into 2026), and licensed electrical labor (4–5 yr
licensure pipeline; ~30% of union electricians at/near retirement). Net:
only 5 of 16 announced-2026 GW under construction; 30–50% of 2026 projects
expected delayed/cancelled; TD haircuts announced capex to 60–70%
deliverable. **Co-binding:** memory (HBM sold out 2026; 3:1 wafer
cannibalization of conventional DRAM).

**Migration forecast (dated):** as equipment factory expansions land
2027-28, the residual constraint concentrates in the two slowest legs —
interconnect/permitting and labor (licensure cohorts land 2028-2030).

**Honest confidence:** HIGH that power delivery binds now — multiple
independent sources with hard delivery data, one read in full. **LOW on
migration timing.** And the §2 discipline cuts hard here: every layer of
this call is at least partially priced — power since ~2024 (GEV/VRT/CEG
re-rates), memory since late-2025 (MU's violent re-rate then −39%
drawdown), labor entering coverage mid-2026 (FIX ~5x off its 52wk low).
**Phase 1 found a real, deepening constraint and no unpriced way to hold
it at current prices.**

## 2. The worked thesis — FIX (Comfort Systems USA), full text in `theses/FIX.yml`

One sentence: *US DC electrical/mechanical construction stays labor-bound
through ≥2028 because 2024-25 apprentice cohorts cannot hold journeyman
licenses before 2028-2030 while ~30% of incumbents near retirement, so
FIX converts its committed backlog ($11.94B, +99% YoY) at sustained
margins even if capex growth decelerates to consensus (+25% '27/+6% '28).*
Five dated, public, cadenced invalidation triggers armed (backlog level,
margin compression, labor-pipeline unlock, hyperscaler lease-pipeline
drain, prefab/self-perform displacement).

**The adversarial pass — searched for the refutation FIRST; found:**
- **Valuation:** ~46x FY26 consensus at the $1,694.55 reference vs a 22.7x
  five-year median forward multiple; GF-Value overvaluation calls stacked.
- **Insiders:** $59.7M sold in 3 months, zero purchases.
- **Margin base effect:** EBITDA margin 13.3%→18.3%, first >25% GM quarter
  in company history — the mean-reversion bear has a real base.
- **Displacement (the strongest structural counter):** prefab/modular cuts
  on-site labor ~30% and schedules 30–50%; hyperscaler self-perform is the
  fastest-growing build segment (9.3% CAGR). Cuts both ways — FIX owns
  modular capacity — but it attacks the scarce-hours mechanism directly.
- **Demand keystone:** UBS capex path decelerates to +6% by 2028; Goldman
  models multiple compression on any AI slowdown.

**Verdict: survives as `candidate` — mechanism intact (the licensure clock
is a legal fact, not a forecast), price late.** The rebuy test at creation
answers NO at reference price; the thesis earns operator attention on
drawdowns that do not coincide with a trigger firing. This is the §2
machinery working as designed: without it, this file would be a
recommendation; with it, it is a dated, falsifiable watch with five
tripwires.

## 4. Where the schema fought me

1. **`market_error` needed the consensus-dating pair** (`when_bottleneck_
   became_consensus` vs `when_price_moved`) promoted to first-class fields
   — it turned out to be the load-bearing test: it rejected MU outright and
   moderated FIX. The handoff's §2 table had it as a guard; in practice it
   is THE gate.
2. **`disqualifier_check` needed states beyond pass/fail:** `watch`
   (customer concentration is simultaneously the thesis and its gap risk)
   and `unverified` (honesty about cells not yet closed — with the rule
   that they MUST close before any operator entry).
3. **`rebuy_test` needed `answer_at_creation`.** A candidate file with an
   unanswered rebuy question reads as an endorsement. Answering "NO at this
   price" while keeping `candidate` status is the honest shape the original
   schema couldn't express.
4. **The adversarial pass generated a trigger** (T5 displacement, from the
   prefab refutation). Triggers written only from the bull case would have
   missed it — the schema should expect the refutation search to ADD
   triggers, not merely test existing ones.

## 5. Anything suggesting this should not be built

Three honest flags, none fatal:

1. **The structural edge is substantially priced, today.** Phase 1's
   strongest finding is that all three constraint clusters were discovered
   by the market before us (2024 / 2025 / mid-2026). At THIS point in the
   cycle the book's realistic value is **discipline** — dated triggers,
   scheduled rebuy tests, measured dispositions — and readiness for the
   next constraint migration, not discovery alpha on the current one. If
   the operator expected immediate mispricings, this report says plainly:
   none found at current prices.
2. **Common-mode demand risk.** Every layer shares one keystone: AI capex
   durability. A thesis book concentrated in this stack has one macro
   factor wearing thirteen costumes; the UBS/Goldman deceleration path
   hits every thesis at once. Diversification across layers is NOT
   diversification of the keystone.
3. **The §2 asymmetry is real and this session felt it.** Thematic research
   FEELS productive (this was a pleasant session; the 0-for-25 systematic
   record was not). That feeling is the documented danger. The disposition
   ledger is armed from day one (row 1: MU rejected, forward checks
   2027-02-09 / 2028-02-09, reference prices recorded) precisely so the
   feeling gets audited by arithmetic.

**Recommendation: keep it** — cost is low, the falsification machinery is
in place before any position exists, and the constraint-migration watch
(the one thing nobody can front-run today) is exactly what a multi-year
book is for. But its first performance review should score dispositions,
not narratives.
